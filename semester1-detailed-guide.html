Physical Science Semester 1 - Detailed Teacher's Guide

Modules 1-8 (Weeks 1-16) - Complete with Experiments and Materials

Weeks 1-2: Module 1 - Scientific Method

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand scientific method steps
  • Practice measurement and precision
  • Learn lab safety protocols
  • Master significant figures

Week 1A: Introduction

Content: Course intro, scientific method, lab safety, basic measurements

📦 Materials: Rulers, balances, graduated cylinders, safety equipment

Week 1B: Measurement & Data Analysis

🔬 Experiment 1.1: Measurement Precision Investigation
  • Multiple measurements by different students
  • Calculate average, range, standard deviation
  • Compare precision of different tools
🔬 Experiment 1.2: Accuracy Determination
  • Measure objects with known dimensions
  • Calculate percent error
  • Identify sources of error
📦 Materials: Calipers, objects with known dimensions, calculators
📝 Assessment: Module 1 Quiz (scientific method, measurement, significant figures); Lab report (precision and accuracy analysis)

Weeks 3-4: Module 2 - Matter & Properties

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Three states of matter and kinetic molecular theory
  • Physical vs chemical properties and changes
  • Density calculations and applications

Week 2A: States of Matter

Content: Solid, liquid, gas properties; kinetic molecular theory; phase changes

📦 Materials: Ice, hot plate, beakers, thermometers

Week 2B: Properties & Density

🔬 Experiment 2.1: Density Determination Lab
  • Measure mass and volume of objects
  • Calculate density using D = m/V
  • Create density tower with liquids
  • Identify unknown substances
🔬 Experiment 2.2: Physical vs Chemical Changes
  • Observe melting, dissolving, burning
  • Classify changes as physical or chemical
  • Identify evidence of chemical reactions
📦 Materials: Balances, graduated cylinders, corn syrup, vegetable oil, water, food coloring
📝 Assessment: Module 2 Quiz (states of matter, properties, density); Density lab report

Weeks 5-6: Module 3 - Atomic Structure

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Historical atomic theory development
  • Subatomic particles and properties
  • Atomic number, mass number, isotopes
  • Periodic table organization

Week 3A: Atomic Theory

Content: Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford models; subatomic particles; atomic structure

📦 Materials: Atomic model kits, periodic tables, colored manipulatives

Week 3B: Electrons & Periodic Trends

🔬 Experiment 3.1: Flame Test Lab
  • Test metal salts in flame
  • Observe characteristic colors
  • Connect to electron energy transitions
  • Identify unknown metal samples
🔬 Experiment 3.2: Isotope Investigations
  • Calculate average atomic mass
  • Model isotopes with different neutrons
  • Understand radioactive vs stable isotopes
📦 Materials: Bunsen burners, metal salt samples (NaCl, CaCl₂, LiCl), wooden splints, safety equipment
📝 Assessment: Module 3 Quiz (atomic theory, subatomic particles); Atomic structure project

Weeks 7-8: Module 4 - Chemical Bonding

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Ionic vs covalent bonding mechanisms
  • Electronegativity and bond polarity
  • Lewis dot structures
  • Molecular geometry (VSEPR theory)

Week 4A: Types of Bonds

Content: Electron transfer vs sharing; electronegativity; Lewis structures

📦 Materials: Molecular model kits, electronegativity charts, periodic tables

Week 4B: Molecular Geometry & Properties

🔬 Experiment 4.1: Ionic vs Covalent Properties
  • Test electrical conductivity
  • Observe solubility in solvents
  • Compare melting points
  • Relate properties to bonding type
🔬 Experiment 4.2: Molecular Model Building
  • Build 3D models of molecules
  • Determine molecular geometry
  • Predict polarity based on shape
📦 Materials: Conductivity tester, ionic/covalent compounds, molecular model kits
📝 Assessment: Module 4 Quiz (bonding types, Lewis structures, geometry); Molecular modeling project

Weeks 9-10: Module 5 - Chemical Reactions

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Classify chemical reactions by type
  • Balance chemical equations
  • Understand factors affecting reaction rates
  • Recognize catalysts and their effects

Week 5A: Reaction Types

Content: Synthesis, decomposition, replacement reactions; equation balancing

📦 Materials: Magnesium ribbon, HCl, baking soda, vinegar, test tubes

Week 5B: Reaction Rates & Energy

🔬 Experiment 5.1: Reaction Rate Investigation
  • Test temperature effects on Alka-Seltzer dissolution
  • Investigate concentration effects
  • Examine surface area effects
  • Time reactions and graph results
🔬 Experiment 5.2: Catalyst Effects Lab
  • H₂O₂ decomposition with/without MnO₂
  • Observe gas production rates
  • Test different catalysts
📦 Materials: Alka-Seltzer, thermometers, stopwatches, H₂O₂, MnO₂, graduated cylinders
📝 Assessment: Module 5 Quiz (reaction types, equation balancing, rates); Reaction lab report

Weeks 11-12: Module 6 - Motion & Forces

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Define position, velocity, acceleration
  • Interpret motion graphs
  • Identify types of forces
  • Calculate net force

Week 6A: Describing Motion

Content: Motion terms; velocity and acceleration calculations; motion graphs

📦 Materials: Stopwatches, meter sticks, rolling balls, motion sensors (optional)

Week 6B: Forces & Net Force

🔬 Experiment 6.1: Force Measurement Lab
  • Use spring scales to measure forces
  • Create force diagrams
  • Calculate net force in multiple directions
🔬 Experiment 6.2: Friction Investigation
  • Measure static and kinetic friction
  • Test different surface materials
  • Investigate factors affecting friction
📦 Materials: Spring scales, wooden blocks, various surfaces, masses
📝 Assessment: Module 6 Quiz (motion, forces, graphs); Motion and forces project

Weeks 13-14: Module 7 - Newton's Laws

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand Newton's three laws of motion
  • Apply F = ma in calculations
  • Identify action-reaction pairs
  • Connect laws to real-world examples

Week 7A: First & Second Laws

Content: Inertia concepts; F = ma relationship; real-world applications

📦 Materials: Carts, masses, demonstration materials for inertia

Week 7B: Third Law & Applications

🔬 Experiment 7.1: Cart and Mass Acceleration Lab
  • Vary mass and measure acceleration
  • Verify F = ma relationship
  • Graph force vs acceleration
🔬 Experiment 7.2: Action-Reaction Demonstrations
  • Balloon rockets
  • Cart pushing experiments
  • Identify action-reaction pairs
📦 Materials: Carts, masses, balloons, string, force sensors
📝 Assessment: Module 7 Quiz (Newton's laws, F=ma calculations); Newton's laws project

Weeks 15-16: Module 8 - Energy & Work

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Calculate kinetic and potential energy
  • Understand energy transformations
  • Apply work and power formulas
  • Verify conservation of energy

Week 8A: Forms of Energy

Content: KE and PE definitions; energy calculations; energy transformations

📦 Materials: Pendulums, springs, masses, rulers for height measurements

Week 8B: Work, Power & Conservation

🔬 Experiment 8.1: Pendulum Energy Conservation
  • Measure pendulum height and speed
  • Calculate PE and KE at different points
  • Verify energy conservation
🔬 Experiment 8.2: Inclined Plane Work and Energy
  • Measure work done on inclined plane
  • Calculate mechanical advantage
  • Determine efficiency
📦 Materials: Pendulums, inclined planes, spring scales, masses, stopwatches
📝 Assessment: Module 8 Quiz (energy, work, power); Energy lab report; Semester 1 Final Review

📊 Semester 1 Assessment Summary

  • 8 Module Quizzes - One per module
  • 16+ Lab Experiments - Hands-on investigations
  • 4 Major Lab Reports - Detailed analysis
  • 2 Integrated Projects - Combining modules
  • Semester 1 Review - Comprehensive preparation
