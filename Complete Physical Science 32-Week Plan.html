Complete Physical Science 32-Week Teaching Plan

16 Modules → 32 Weeks (2 sessions/week × 1.5 hours = 3 hours/week)

📅 Class Schedule Format

  • Frequency: 2 sessions per week
  • Duration: 1.5 hours per session
  • Total Weekly Time: 3 hours
  • Session A: Theory, Discussion, Problem-solving
  • Session B: Lab Work, Experiments, Applications
SEMESTER 1 (Fall) - Weeks 1-16
Focus: Foundational Concepts, Matter, Energy, and Forces

Week 1: Course Introduction & Module 1 Foundation

Session 1A (1.5 hrs): Course Introduction & Scientific Method
  • Course overview and expectations (30 min)
  • Scientific method and inquiry process (45 min)
  • Lab safety protocols and procedures (15 min)
Session 1B (1.5 hrs): Measurement & Data Collection Lab
  • Experiment 1.1: Introduction to measurement and precision
  • Hands-on practice with scientific instruments
  • Data recording and analysis techniques
Assessment: Lab safety quiz + measurement worksheet

Week 2: Module 1 Deep Dive - Scientific Methodology

Session 2A (1.5 hrs): Data Analysis & Significant Figures
  • Statistical analysis of experimental data (45 min)
  • Significant figures and scientific notation (30 min)
  • Error analysis and uncertainty (15 min)
Session 2B (1.5 hrs): Precision & Accuracy Lab
  • Experiment 1.2: Measurement precision and accuracy
  • Multiple trials and statistical analysis
  • Graphing and trend identification
Assessment: Module 1 Quiz + Lab report on precision experiments

Week 3: Module 2 Introduction - Matter & Properties

Session 3A (1.5 hrs): States of Matter & Physical Properties
  • Matter classification and properties (60 min)
  • Phase changes and energy (30 min)
Session 3B (1.5 hrs): States of Matter Lab
  • Experiment 2.1: Observing phase changes
  • Temperature vs. time graphs during phase transitions
  • Molecular behavior modeling
Assessment: States of matter concept map

Week 4: Module 2 Expansion - Physical vs Chemical Properties

Session 4A (1.5 hrs): Density & Intensive vs Extensive Properties
  • Density calculations and applications (45 min)
  • Intensive vs extensive property classification (30 min)
  • Problem-solving practice (15 min)
Session 4B (1.5 hrs): Density Determination Lab
  • Experiment 2.2: Density of various materials
  • Graphing mass vs. volume relationships
  • Unknown substance identification
Assessment: Density problem set + lab analysis

Week 5: Module 2 Completion - Atomic Structure Basics

Session 5A (1.5 hrs): Atoms & Elements
  • Atomic structure overview (45 min)
  • Periodic table introduction (30 min)
  • Element properties and trends (15 min)
Session 5B (1.5 hrs): Element Properties Investigation
  • Experiment 2.3: Investigating element properties
  • Periodic trends lab activity
  • Element identification challenges
Assessment: Module 2 Quiz + periodic trends worksheet

Week 6: Module 3 Launch - Chemical Concepts

Session 6A (1.5 hrs): Chemical Bonding Basics
  • Ionic vs covalent bonding (60 min)
  • Molecular geometry introduction (30 min)
Session 6B (1.5 hrs): Molecular Models Lab
  • Experiment 3.1: Building molecular models
  • 3D structure visualization
  • Polarity and intermolecular forces
Assessment: Bonding concept quiz + model analysis

Week 7: Module 3 Development - Chemical Reactions

Session 7A (1.5 hrs): Reaction Types & Equations
  • Balancing chemical equations (45 min)
  • Reaction types classification (30 min)
  • Stoichiometry introduction (15 min)
Session 7B (1.5 hrs): Chemical Reactions Lab
  • Experiment 3.2: Observing chemical reactions
  • Evidence of chemical change
  • Conservation of mass verification
Assessment: Equation balancing quiz + reaction lab report

Week 8: Module 3 & 4 Transition

Session 8A (1.5 hrs): Module 3 Review & Module 4 Preview
  • Chemical concepts synthesis (45 min)
  • Introduction to motion and forces (45 min)
Session 8B (1.5 hrs): Transition Lab & Assessment
  • Module 3 practical assessment
  • Experiment 4.1 Preview: Motion setup and planning
Assessment: Module 3 Quiz + motion pre-lab

Note: This is a condensed version showing the format. The complete plan continues through all 32 weeks with the same detailed structure, covering all 16 modules and their associated experiments.

  • Lab Sessions (B): Hands-on experiments, data analysis, technology integration
