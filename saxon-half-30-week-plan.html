Saxon Math 1/2 Second Edition - 30 Week Teaching Plan

Course Overview

Grade Level: Kindergarten - 1st Grade

Duration: 30 weeks (150 lessons)

Lessons per week: 5

Focus: Number recognition, counting, basic addition/subtraction, patterns, measurement, and geometry foundations
