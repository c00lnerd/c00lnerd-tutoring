Saxon Algebra 1 30-Week Plan

Building Strong Mathematical Foundations with Integrated Geometry

✅ Excellent Choice!

Saxon Algebra 1 (3rd Edition) is the perfect foundation for your student. This curriculum:

  • Builds essential algebraic skills systematically
  • Includes integrated geometry concepts (earning 1/4 geometry credit)
  • Prepares students for future success in Saxon Geometry
  • Uses incremental development with continuous review
  • Provides a solid foundation for higher mathematics

🎯 Why This Approach Works

  • Natural Progression: Algebra and geometry concepts are introduced together as they naturally support each other
  • Confidence Building: Student develops algebraic fluency before tackling advanced geometric proofs
  • Future Success: Creates the perfect foundation for Saxon Geometry next year
  • Comprehensive Coverage: Covers all standard Algebra 1 topics plus essential geometry

🎯 Tutoring Success Strategies

  • Incremental Approach: Follow Saxon's method - introduce new concepts gradually while reviewing previous material
  • Daily Practice: Encourage consistent daily work with mixed practice sets
  • Geometry Emphasis: Highlight geometry concepts as they appear to prepare for future geometry study
  • Problem-Solving Focus: Spend extra time on word problems and real-world applications
  • Mastery Checks: Regular assessment to ensure solid understanding before moving forward
  • Visual Learning: Use graphs, diagrams, and manipulatives to support algebraic concepts
  • Connection Building: Help student see connections between algebra and geometry throughout

📚 Course Structure Summary

Total Lessons: 120 lessons across 30 weeks (4 lessons per week)

Geometry Integration: Approximately 25% geometry content woven throughout

Assessment: 30 tests plus regular problem sets and cumulative reviews

Time Commitment: Approximately 1.5 hours per tutoring session

Preparation for: Saxon Geometry, Algebra 2, or other advanced mathematics
