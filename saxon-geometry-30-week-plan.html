Saxon Geometry 30-Week Plan

Comprehensive tutoring schedule for Saxon Geometry Student Text

⚠️ Important Prerequisite Warning

Saxon Geometry is designed for students who have completed Algebra I and preferably Algebra II. The publisher recommends taking it after Algebra 2 because:

  • Heavy integration of algebraic concepts throughout geometry problems
  • Formal proof requirements that need strong algebraic manipulation skills
  • Complex problem-solving requiring equation-solving abilities
  • Advanced logical reasoning building on algebraic foundations

Recommendation: Consider starting with Saxon Algebra 1 (which includes integrated geometry) or ensure the student has strong pre-algebra skills before attempting this course.

🎯 Tutoring Recommendations

  • Pre-Assessment: Test student's comfort with solving linear equations, working with variables, and basic algebraic manipulation
  • Algebra Review: Dedicate 10-15 minutes each session to reviewing essential algebra skills as they appear in geometry problems
  • Proof Preparation: Start with very simple logical reasoning exercises before formal two-column proofs
  • Visual Learning: Use manipulatives, drawings, and geometric software to support conceptual understanding
  • Pacing Flexibility: Be prepared to slow down on algebraically intensive lessons and spend extra time on foundational concepts
  • Alternative Approach: Consider using Saxon Algebra 1 (3rd edition) first, which integrates geometry naturally and builds the necessary algebraic foundation

📚 Course Structure Summary

Total Lessons: 120 lessons across 30 weeks (4 lessons per week)

Additional Components: 12 Investigations + 15 Labs

Assessment: Regular problem sets, chapter tests, and cumulative reviews

Time Commitment: Approximately 1.5 hours per tutoring session
