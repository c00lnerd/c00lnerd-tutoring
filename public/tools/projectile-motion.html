📚 Kinematic Equations Reference

v = v₀ + gt
x = v₀t + ½gt²
v² = v₀² + 2gx
x = ½(v₀ + v)t

Where: v₀ = initial velocity, v = final velocity, g = acceleration due to gravity (9.81 m/s²), t = time, x = displacement
