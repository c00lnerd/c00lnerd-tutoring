🚫 Problems with Flat Earth Moon Model:

  • Moon Phases Don't Work: On a flat earth, the moon would need to be self-illuminating or have impossible lighting to create phases
  • Same Moon Phase Globally: Everyone on earth would see the same moon phase simultaneously, which contradicts reality
  • No Lunar Eclipses: Earth's shadow couldn't create lunar eclipses in this model
  • Moon Size Changes: The moon would appear dramatically different sizes from different locations
  • Impossible Orbit: No physical mechanism could maintain the moon's circular path above a flat surface
  • Tidal Forces: Tides would behave completely differently and inconsistently across the flat earth

✅ Reality: Spherical Earth Model

In reality, the Earth is a sphere, and the moon orbits around it. Moon phases are created by the changing angles of sunlight hitting the moon as viewed from Earth. This spherical model perfectly explains:

  • Why moon phases progress predictably
  • Why lunar eclipses occur when Earth blocks sunlight
  • Why the moon appears the same size globally
  • How tidal forces work consistently
  • Why we can predict moon positions centuries in advance
