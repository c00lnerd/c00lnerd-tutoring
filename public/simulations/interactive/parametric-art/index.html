📚 Understanding Parametric Equations

Current Equations:
x(t) = a × sin(b × t + c) × cos(d × t)
y(t) = a × cos(b × t + c) × sin(d × t)

🔢 What are Parametric Equations?

Parametric equations express coordinates as functions of a parameter (usually t for time). Instead of y = f(x), we have x = f(t) and y = g(t), allowing us to create curves that loop, spiral, and create complex patterns impossible with regular functions.

🌊 Trigonometric Functions

Sine and cosine functions create smooth, periodic motion. When combined with different frequencies and phases, they produce beautiful, flowing patterns. The parameters a, b, c, and d control amplitude, frequency, phase, and interaction between the x and y components.

🎨 Mathematics in Art

These patterns appear in nature: flower petals, seashells, galaxies, and sound waves. By adjusting parameters, we can mimic natural forms or create entirely new artistic expressions, showing how mathematics underlies beauty in both nature and art.

🔬 Real-World Applications

Parametric equations are used in computer graphics, animation, robotics (robot arm movements), physics (particle trajectories), and engineering (cam design, gear teeth). Understanding them opens doors to advanced mathematics and engineering careers.
