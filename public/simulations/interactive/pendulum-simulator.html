🧮 Physics Behind the Simulation

This simulation uses the exact differential equation for pendulum motion:

d²θ/dt² = -(g/L)sin(θ) - b(dθ/dt)

Where θ is angle, g is gravity (9.81 m/s²), L is length, and b is damping coefficient. No small angle approximation!
