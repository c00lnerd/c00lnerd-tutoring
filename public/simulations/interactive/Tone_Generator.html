🔬 Usage Instructions

For FFT Testing: Use this generator as an audio source while running the FFT Heterodyne Simulation in another tab.

Waveforms:

  • Sine: Pure tone, single frequency peak in FFT
  • Square: Rich harmonics at odd multiples of fundamental
  • Sawtooth: All harmonics decreasing in amplitude
  • Triangle: Odd harmonics with faster rolloff than square
  • White Noise: Flat spectrum across all frequencies
  • Pink Noise: 1/f spectrum, more energy at low frequencies

Presets: Try different presets to test various FFT scenarios like chord analysis, harmonic detection, and interference patterns.
