Physical Science Semester 2 - Detailed Teacher's Guide

Modules 9-15 (Weeks 17-32) - Complete with Experiments and Materials

Weeks 17-18: Module 9 - Waves & Sound

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand wave properties: wavelength, frequency, amplitude
  • Calculate wave speed using v = fλ
  • Explain sound production and propagation
  • Investigate wave behaviors: reflection, refraction, interference

Week 9A: Wave Properties

Content: Wave characteristics; wave speed calculations; reflection and refraction basics

📦 Materials: Slinkies, wave tanks (or large shallow pans), rulers, stopwatches

Week 9B: Sound Waves & Applications

🔬 Experiment 9.1: Sound Frequency and Pitch Lab
  • Use tuning forks of different frequencies
  • Measure wavelength in air
  • Calculate speed of sound
  • Investigate pitch vs frequency relationship
🔬 Experiment 9.2: Sound Wave Interference
  • Create constructive and destructive interference
  • Use two speakers with same frequency
  • Map areas of loud and quiet sound
📦 Materials: Tuning forks (various frequencies), resonance tubes, speakers, signal generator, microphones
📝 Assessment: Module 9 Quiz (wave properties, sound calculations); Wave analysis project

Weeks 19-20: Module 10 - Light & Electromagnetic Spectrum

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand light as electromagnetic radiation
  • Apply laws of reflection and refraction
  • Explore the electromagnetic spectrum
  • Connect wavelength to energy and applications

Week 10A: Properties of Light

Content: Light wave properties; reflection and refraction laws; ray diagrams; lens and mirror basics

📦 Materials: Laser pointers, mirrors, prisms, lenses, protractors, ray boxes

Week 10B: Electromagnetic Spectrum & Applications

🔬 Experiment 10.1: Reflection and Refraction Lab
  • Measure angles of incidence and reflection
  • Verify law of reflection
  • Investigate refraction through different materials
  • Calculate index of refraction
🔬 Experiment 10.2: Color and Wavelength Investigation
  • Use prism to separate white light
  • Measure wavelengths of different colors
  • Investigate color mixing and filters
📦 Materials: Prisms, white light sources, colored filters, diffraction gratings, rulers
📝 Assessment: Module 10 Quiz (light properties, EM spectrum); Light and optics project

Weeks 21-22: Module 11 - Electricity & Magnetism

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand electric charge and electric fields
  • Analyze series and parallel circuits
  • Apply Ohm's Law (V = IR)
  • Explore magnetic fields and electromagnetic induction

Week 11A: Electric Charge & Current

Content: Static electricity; electric fields; simple circuits; Ohm's Law introduction

📦 Materials: Van de Graaff generator, balloons, electroscopes, simple circuit components

Week 11B: Circuits & Magnetism

🔬 Experiment 11.1: Circuit Building and Analysis
  • Build series and parallel circuits
  • Measure voltage, current, and resistance
  • Verify Ohm's Law experimentally
  • Compare circuit types
🔬 Experiment 11.2: Electromagnet Investigation
  • Build electromagnets with different coil turns
  • Test magnetic field strength
  • Investigate factors affecting electromagnet strength
📦 Materials: Batteries, wires, resistors, LEDs, multimeters, iron nails, copper wire, compasses
📝 Assessment: Module 11 Quiz (electricity, circuits, magnetism); Circuit design project

Weeks 23-24: Module 12 - Earth's Interior

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Describe Earth's layers and their properties
  • Explain plate tectonics theory
  • Understand earthquake and volcano formation
  • Analyze seismic wave data

Week 12A: Earth's Structure & Layers

Content: Crust, mantle, outer core, inner core; evidence for Earth's structure; continental drift

📦 Materials: Earth layer models, density materials, maps showing plate boundaries

Week 12B: Plate Tectonics & Geological Processes

🔬 Experiment 12.1: Plate Boundary Modeling
  • Model divergent boundaries with clay
  • Simulate convergent boundary collisions
  • Demonstrate transform boundary motion
  • Connect to real-world examples
🔬 Experiment 12.2: Earthquake Wave Simulation
  • Use slinky to model P and S waves
  • Measure wave speeds through different materials
  • Triangulate earthquake epicenter using data
📦 Materials: Clay, slinkies, world maps, earthquake data, rulers, compasses
📝 Assessment: Module 12 Quiz (Earth structure, plate tectonics); Earth structure project

Weeks 25-26: Module 13 - Earth's Atmosphere & Beyond

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Identify atmospheric layers and composition
  • Understand weather patterns and climate
  • Explore solar system structure and scale
  • Investigate stellar evolution basics

Week 13A: Atmospheric Structure & Weather

Content: Atmosphere layers; pressure and temperature changes; weather vs climate

📦 Materials: Barometers, weather maps, atmospheric pressure demonstrations

Week 13B: Solar System & Space Science

🔬 Experiment 13.1: Solar System Scale Model
  • Calculate scaled distances between planets
  • Create to-scale model in hallway or outdoors
  • Compare planet sizes and distances
  • Understand vast scale of space
🔬 Experiment 13.2: Crater Formation Investigation
  • Drop objects into flour/sand to create craters
  • Vary height, mass, and angle
  • Measure crater diameter and depth
  • Compare to real lunar and planetary craters
📦 Materials: Calculators, measuring tapes, flour, sand, various balls, rulers, lunar/planetary images
📝 Assessment: Module 13 Quiz (atmosphere, weather, solar system); Astronomy project

Weeks 27-28: Module 14 - Chemistry & Physics in Life Sciences

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Understand organic chemistry and carbon compounds
  • Identify biochemical molecules and their functions
  • Apply Bernoulli's principle to flight
  • Explore physics applications in medicine and forensics

Week 14A: Biochemistry & Organic Chemistry

Content: Carbon bonding; biochemical compounds; photosynthesis and cellular respiration

📦 Materials: Molecular models, food samples for testing, iodine solution, Benedict's solution

Week 14B: Physics Applications in Life Sciences

🔬 Experiment 14.1: Bernoulli's Principle Demonstration
  • Use funnel and ping-pong ball setup
  • Investigate airfoil shapes and lift
  • Test different wing designs
  • Connect to airplane flight principles
🔬 Experiment 14.2: Biochemistry Investigations
  • Test foods for starch, sugar, protein
  • Investigate enzyme activity
  • Explore pH effects on biological systems
📦 Materials: Hair dryer, funnel, ping-pong balls, cardboard, food samples, test reagents, pH strips
📝 Assessment: Module 14 Quiz (biochemistry, physics in life); Life science applications project

Weeks 29-30: Module 15 - Physical Science Research

🎯 Learning Objectives:

  • Develop scientific research questions
  • Evaluate source credibility and bias
  • Design and conduct independent investigations
  • Present findings effectively

Week 15A: Research Methods & Source Evaluation

Content: Research question development; literature review; source credibility evaluation

📦 Materials: Computers/tablets for research, research evaluation rubrics, example sources

Week 15B: Research Project Work & Presentation Prep

Content: Independent research time; data collection and analysis; presentation preparation

🔬 Independent Research Projects:
  • Students choose topics from any physical science area
  • Design experiments or conduct literature reviews
  • Collect and analyze data
  • Prepare presentations for final weeks
📦 Materials: Varies by student project; presentation materials (poster board, computers, etc.)
📝 Assessment: Module 15 Quiz (research methods); Research proposal; Begin final projects

Weeks 31-32: Course Synthesis & Final Assessment

Week 31: Research Presentations & Course Review

Content: Student research presentations; comprehensive course review; final exam preparation

  • Individual student presentations (3-4 students, 10 min each)
  • Peer feedback and discussion
  • Major concept connections across all modules
  • Final exam format review

Week 32: Final Exam & Course Celebration

Content: Comprehensive final examination; course reflection; celebration of achievements

  • Comprehensive final exam (open book/notes format)
  • Covers all 15 modules with emphasis on connections
  • Course reflection and learning assessment
  • Celebration of learning journey
📝 Final Assessment: Final Exam, Research project presentations, Course portfolio
